    Beginner Tutorial

    1 / 7

    Camera controls

    Drag with the left mouse button to orbit. Shift-drag to pan, and use the scroll wheel to zoom. On a touchscreen, use one finger to orbit and two fingers to pan or pinch to zoom.

    2 / 7

    Choosing your desk

    Choose Desks in the library. Open Options to choose a size or enter custom dimensions in inches. Use desk replaces the current desk.

    3 / 7

    Adding objects

    Choose a category or search the library, then click Add. Select an object in the workspace to see its controls. Delete / Backspace removes it when the workspace has focus.

    4 / 7

    Replacing objects

    Select an object, then use Replace selected on a library card. Replacement keeps its X/Z location; height and rotation use the new object’s authored defaults.

    5 / 7

    Moving objects

    Click an object and drag its handles. Use Move / Rotate in the selected panel, or press R while the workspace has focus.

    6 / 7

    Adjusting controls

    Use X / Y / Z to toggle the respective axes. Use + / − to change handle size. These controls are also available in the selected panel.

    7 / 7

    Screenshot and share

    Press C in the workspace to deselect. Take a screenshot using your operating system’s screenshot tool. Share / Save generates a link to reopen the setup; generate a new link after edits.
